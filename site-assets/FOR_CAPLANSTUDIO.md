# Founding Tide - status for caplanstudio.com

**Stage:** pre-alpha (0.3.0-alpha.1). Playable end to end; parts are still unplaytested. Not ready for outside players yet.
**Time in development:** first commit 2025-11-16 (about 11 months); 153 commits over 38 working days.
**Shots:** real frames from the game's own build (a watched simulation run, 1920x1080, nothing edited). Placeholder low-poly art. The "Development Build" mark and the bottom-left caption come from that test build and are not part of the game.

- hero-01.png - Day 7: a walled village of 25 colonists around the campfire.
- hero-02.png - Day 9: the same village, stone and wood stockpiles growing.
- shot-02-early-camp.png - Day 2: the first tents and huts going up.
- shot-03-raid-night.png - Night 11: raid warning ("Raiders will land from the south-west").

**What it is (draft):** a survival strategy game on a raid clock. You are a castaway who builds a colony, researches tools, walls the camp and arms a militia to survive 30 days of raids.
**Built:** colonist AI, walls and gates, research and crafting, raids, fog of war, minimap, rival colonies, a balance simulator.
**Still to build for alpha:** births, fishing and boar, an Endless mode, save/load.
