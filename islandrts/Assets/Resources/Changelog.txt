## 2026-09-17 — A shorter changelog
Every entry now folds to a title and a one-line summary. Click it for the details.
- The changelog was seventy-odd entries long. It is now a handful of themed
  entries that say where the game stands, and new changes go on top from here.
- The newest entry opens on its own; click any other to unfold it, and again
  to fold it back.

## 2026-09-17 — The militia, the levy and the line
Warriors take a stance, form up, and every spare weapon arms a colonist when the bell rings.
- A Stance row (and F5, F8, F9) gives the militia one order. Defensive holds a
  line inside the gate the raiders are heading for and fights what reaches it.
  Offensive advances in formation and charges. Follow escorts your castaway.
- Formations: Line, Wedge or Ring, or Auto to match the stance. Each warrior owns
  a place and keeps it into the fight. Warriors on the same raider spread out
  around it instead of queueing.
- The levy: every weapon left in the stockpile arms a colonist when raiders
  threaten the colony or you ring the bell (F10). They fight as warriors, then
  half a minute after the last threat they put the weapon back and return to
  their job. No food cost, no bigger raids.
- Archers: Bowyery opens the Bow (12 damage at range 9). An archer holds back
  and shoots over your walls. Iron Work opens the Iron Spear (35 damage);
  spearmen swap up to it on their own in peacetime.
- A raid night lasts until the last raider is dead, the calendar reads "Raid
  repelled" when it is, and "Raiders lurking" when they are ashore but idle.
  Raiders never come two nights running; the colony is owed two quiet nights.
- Raiders never land inside a closed wall ring, never get moved through a wall,
  and attack the wall rather than staring at a warrior behind it. Chewed gates
  get repaired like walls.
- On Hard and Brutal the raids start at Normal size and grow to full strength
  over the first week. A big hoard draws bigger raids only up to a point, and
  walls draw half the attention they used to.
- The Watchtower is vision only for now: it sees furthest and shows a raid
  coming. Towers that shoot are a later upgrade.

## 2026-09-17 — Colonists with names, beds and manners
Survivors have names and traits, sleep from midnight to dawn, and no longer jam in doorways.
- Every survivor lands with a name and a trait (Night Owl, Early Riser, Hardy,
  Lazy, Steady) that shifts their hours. The People list on the Colonists tab
  shows who is doing what.
- Colonists work into the evening and sleep from midnight to dawn: in their hut
  if they have one, beside the fire if not. The founding crew moves into the
  first hut with room. A load in hand is delivered before bed.
- Idle colonists do whatever the colony needs: build a waiting site, work a
  bench with a queue, repair, then tidy the beach. Four Priority sliders order
  that. Builders, Crafters and Repairers are specialists pinned to one trade.
- A job change sends the colonist to the fire first to hand in and gear up.
  Idle colonists stroll the village by day and go home at night, never standing
  in a gateway or on the wall line.
- People give way to each other by a fixed rule (cargo first, then whoever is
  already in the gateway), your castaway has right of way, and a stuck unit
  recovers in steps: a sidestep, a fresh path, a squeeze through.
- Everyone eats one food a day. A quarter day without food is Hungry (work at
  60%, no arrivals); a full day is Starving (one colonist leaves per day).
  Your castaway does not eat.
- Survivors come ashore on their own while there is a bed; housing is the only
  limit on the colony, and a warrior needs a bed, an idle colonist, a weapon
  and 15 food.

## 2026-09-16 — Neighbours
Turn Rivals on and another shipwreck founds a colony of its own, with opinions about you.
- New Game has a Rivals option, off by default. A third of the way through the
  run a second ship breaks up on a far shore. A banner says so, not where.
- A rival runs itself: it researches, builds huts and a wall ring, crafts spears
  and arms warriors against the same raids you face. Each lands with its own
  temperament: a fortifier, a militarist or a builder of economy.
- The DIPLOMACY screen (Esc, or Neighbours on the bar) shows what each colony
  you have met thinks of you. Propose peace with a gift once a day or declare
  war. Opinion moves on its own: warriors on their side of shared ground and
  blows landed cool it, quiet days and peace warm it.
- Raiders pick a shore each night; the dawn warning says whose. A hostile camp
  with spare warriors lands a party on your beach by day. An ally sends half its
  warriors when raiders reach your fire.
- Land on a hostile neighbour and your warriors besiege the camp: huts and works,
  then the wall, then the fire. A fire your warriors put out spills its whole
  hoard onto the ground for anyone to haul.
- Every colony works the ground around its own fire first and reaches further
  only when its own patch is bare.

## 2026-09-16 — Building, research and the ship home
Research teaches, crafting supplies; benches need hands; the Shipyard is the way off the island.
- The campfire panel has Colonists, Stockpile, Craft, Research and Queue tabs.
  A bench only works while someone stands at it, and several pairs of hands
  share one: queue five spears and every idle colonist who comes takes one.
- Finishing a research puts its tool in your hands (axe, pick, mallet, fishing
  spear, metal pick). The Workshop (build key 5) makes tools and weapons twice
  as fast. Costs are paid when an item finishes; a short queue waits, never fails.
- Shipwright, after Iron Work, opens the Shipyard (key 6, beach only, 200 wood
  120 stone 30 metal). Click it to set sail: the run ends as an escape, and you
  can keep playing afterwards.
- The Storehouse (key 7, after Storage Pits) is a drop-off: colonists carry to
  whichever of the fire or a Storehouse is nearer, and each adds twenty items
  of room. The stockpile holds 60 to start; Storage Pits and Racks and Baskets
  raise it.
- Metal is the fourth resource, mined from ore boulders, spent on Iron Work and
  Iron Spears. The wreck's crates (6 food) and barrels (5 wood) can be hauled
  home, and workers shed sticks and chunks around what they work.
- Walls are drawn click to click (L-shaped, or Shift for a staircase), G turns a
  wall into a gate, Delete or X demolishes for half back. Buildings flatten a
  pad and sit flush on slopes. F2 shows the buildable grid.

## 2026-09-13 — Your castaway
You are one named survivor for the whole run: fetch, harvest, build and work benches by right-click.
- Name your castaway at the start. They never turn into a colonist, can be
  knocked out but not killed, and recover at the fire. Space centres on them.
- Right-click is the smart command: fetch a pickup, work a tree, bush or rock by
  hand (with the right tool), deposit at the fire and work its queue, work a
  bench, raise a construction site, or just walk. A six-slot inventory sits at
  the bottom of the screen.
- Hold Shift and right-click to queue orders; a green ring marks the click and
  a trail shows the path. Hold Shift while placing a building to place the
  next one straight away.
- Right-click the ground beside the fire and your castaway walks there, emptying
  their hands as they pass. Right-click a Storehouse to empty them there.
- Your castaway building a site is the way out of a stuck colony: if every
  colonist is on a gathering job, nobody else will.

## 2026-09-12 — Fog, minimap and the view
The island starts dark, a minimap sits top-right, and nothing hides your people.
- Fog of war: ground clears once one of yours has seen it and dims when nobody
  is watching. Colonists only gather from what the colony has found, warriors
  only fight raiders something of yours can see, and you cannot build in the
  dark. A raid in the dark is an ambush; a Watchtower sees it coming.
- The minimap marks buildings, colonists, warriors, your castaway and raiders on
  watched ground, pulses red where a raid lands, and moves the camera on click.
- A tree, building or wall between the camera and one of your people opens a
  soft window right where they are instead of fading. Hovering a node or a
  building warms it with a golden glow.
- Each dawn the sky rolls sunny to cloudy; cloud shade drifts over the island.
  Options > Video has Low to Ultra graphics presets. Shadows fit what is on
  screen and no longer crawl while you pan.
- Middle-mouse drag tilts and rotates the camera. A sun and moon dial shows how
  long until the raiders land. The main menu shows the build's version and
  the date of the newest note here.

## 2026-09-02 — Islands, the calendar and the menus
Every run is a fresh island, thirty days to rescue, with raids announced at dawn.
- New Game asks for island size, terrain style (Rolling, Terraced, Rugged), a
  seed and a difficulty (Peaceful to Brutal, or Custom). Terraced plateaus,
  cliffs climbable by ramps, ponds, stylized water. Restart keeps the island.
- The run is thirty days (twenty on Peaceful and Relaxed). Raiders can land from
  day 3; the odds climb with every quiet night and a raid is certain after five.
  The roll is announced at dawn for the whole day. Victory is the dawn after
  the final day.
- Raid size grows with the day and with how prosperous the colony looks.
  Days are 150 seconds and nights 75; a run is about two hours.
- Main menu, pause menu, Options with four tabs, every gameplay key rebindable,
  and INFORMATION: a field guide with the research tree, recipes, weapons and
  difficulty presets listed from the game's own numbers.
- Every unit, building and resource is low-poly art. Nights are moonlit blue.

## 2025-11-16 — Where it started
The first alpha: an island, a campfire, workers, huts, and enemies at night.
- Workers gathering wood, food and stone, huts, a day and night cycle, warriors
  and enemy waves, walls, gates and watchtowers, 3D audio, and every unit
  deciding what to do by weighing its options rather than following a script.
